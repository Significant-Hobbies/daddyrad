Privacy on this site

This page describes the DaddyRad website. The four Mac apps are local-first and do not include analytics, as described on the home page. Each app's website has its own privacy details.

Website analytics

App Health receives page visits, clicks on the four app links, and technical browser errors from this site. The browser identifier is session-only. The named app-link events contain no form contents or personal profile information.

Newsletter

The optional footer newsletter is operated by SaaS Maker. It requests explicit consent before submitting your email address. You can unsubscribe using the link in any newsletter email. See SaaS Maker's privacy policy for service details.

To ask about information collected through this website, contact DaddyRad through GitHub.
