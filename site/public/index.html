Native Mac utilities that watch over your machine.

Four small apps for the things a Mac keeps to itself — what is slowing it down, what is filling it up, where your attention went, and what your coding agents can see. Local-first. No accounts. No telemetry.

Local-first
Evidence stays on your Mac. No accounts, no sync, no analytics inside the apps.
Measured, not guessed
Every claim comes from a capture you can inspect. Absent evidence is shown as absent.
Review before action
The apps diagnose and surface candidates; stopping a process or deleting a file is always your call.
